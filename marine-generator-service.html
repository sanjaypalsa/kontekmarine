    • Marine Generator Service, Repair & Overhaul

    Reliable solutions to keep your vessel powered, efficient, and always operational.

    Introduction

    A generator failure at sea is not a maintenance issue. It is an emergency. Navigation systems go down, cargo handling stops, and in the worst cases, the vessel is left dark in conditions where every minute matters. Kontek Marine provides Marine & Vessel Genset Generator Service & Maintenance to shipping companies, vessel operators, and offshore contractors who know that proper generator upkeep is not a box to tick, it is what keeps the vessel running.

    Marine generator systems are a dedicated focus at Kontek Marine, not a sideline. Our engineers work on diesel gensets, standby sets, and auxiliary power systems across commercial ships, tankers, offshore support vessels, and ferries. They understand these machines in detail, from fuel injection timing and governor calibration through to load transfer sequencing and alternator testing, and they bring that knowledge to every port call, dry dock job, and emergency callout across India and internationally.

    Services We Offer

    We cover the full scope of marine generator work. Here is what Kontek Marine handles.

    Marine Generator Repair & Overhaul

    Sudden power loss, abnormal vibration, excessive smoke, or recurring fault alarms all need proper investigation. Our Marine Generator Repair & Overhaul Services cover complete engine teardowns, cylinder head rebuilds, piston and liner replacements, bearing renewals, turbocharger overhauls, fuel pump and injector reconditioning, and electrical system repairs. Every overhaul is documented and tested before the generator returns to service.

    Ship & Marine Diesel Generator Servicing

    Our Ship & Marine Diesel Generator Service & Repair programme covers all major brands including Caterpillar, Rolls-Royce-Marine, Abb, Wartsila, Fanuc, Flender,Woodward and Honeywell. Each service visit includes compression testing, fuel and cooling system checks, lube oil analysis, and hours-based component replacement. Detailed service reports are issued after every job.

    Auxiliary Generator Inspection & Testing

    Auxiliary sets are often the last thing on a maintenance schedule until they fail at the wrong moment. Our Marine Auxiliary Generator Service, Inspection & Testing covers load bank testing at rated capacity, insulation resistance checks, voltage and frequency regulation, automatic start and transfer switch verification, and cooling system inspection. Each unit is tested independently.

    Emergency Callouts & Condition Monitoring

    When a generator trips unexpectedly, we mobilise fast. Our emergency teams carry diagnostic tools and critical spares to isolate faults and carry out first-stage repairs on the spot. We also run a condition monitoring programme covering vibration analysis, thermal imaging, lube oil sampling, and exhaust temperature trending, so developing problems are caught before they become emergencies.

    Our Advantages

    Here is what makes working with Kontek Marine different when it comes to generator maintenance.

    Engineers Who Know the Machine

    Our team has hands-on experience across every major marine generator brand in service today. When your engineer arrives, he already knows the specific failure patterns, service tolerances, common problem areas, and the things manufacturers do not always put in the manual for your unit. That knowledge only comes from years of doing the work across different vessel types and different operating conditions.

    Documentation That Holds Up to Scrutiny

    Every job produces a detailed report covering work done, parts replaced, test results, and recommendations. The records support class surveys as well as Port State Control inspections and ISM Code maintenance logs and insurance assessments. Clients regularly tell us our documentation is more thorough than what they received from previous contractors.

    Spares Ready, Response Fast

    Sourcing the right part quickly is where many generator repairs lose days. Kontek Marine keeps stock of fast-moving spares for the most common marine diesel sets at our India-based facility, backed by established OEM distributor relationships worldwide. When a repair needs a part, we locate it fast and get it to the vessel before it holds up the job or delays the sailing schedule.

    Service Planning That Prevents Breakdowns

    Generator failures usually build up over time. We plan maintenance based on running hours, load conditions, and manufacturer limits to prevent unexpected shutdowns, reduce emergency repairs, and keep generators reliable in operation.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    How often should a marine diesel generator be serviced?

    Most marine diesel generators need a running inspection every 250 hours, a minor service at 500 hours, a major service at 1,000 hours, and a full overhaul at 8,000 to 12,000 hours. We follow OEM-recommended intervals for each unit and adjust based on actual condition and service history.

    What brands does Kontek Marine work on?

    We work on Caterpillar, Cummins, MAN, Wartsila, MTU, Volvo Penta, Yanmar, John Deere, Perkins, and Stamford alternators. If your vessel runs a less common set, send us the make and model and we will confirm before you commit.

    Can you carry out generator work during a short port call?

    Yes. A normal generator service or inspection can usually be completed within one day, depending on condition. Our team plans the work as per the vessel schedule, brings required tools, and reports immediately if additional work is needed.

    Does Kontek Marine work outside India?

    Yes. Our generator service teams operate worldwide, including the Middle East, Southeast Asia, East Africa, and Europe. We coordinate in advance with the vessel superintendent and arrive prepared with engineers, tools, and required parts.
