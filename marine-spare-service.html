    Reliable Maintenance & Repair For Ship Generators

    Introduction

    Kontek Marine functions as a reliable marine spare parts supplier which serves vessel operators and shipping companies and shipyards throughout India and international markets. We supply and source parts for all types of vessels which include small workboats and large ocean-going ships. When fast part replacement is needed, we act instantly. A delayed spare can hold a vessel in port for days and cost far more than the part itself. That is why operators need Marine, Ship & Boat Spare Parts Suppliers who keep real stock, know what they are selling, and deliver when it counts. Kontek Marine is set up to do exactly that.

    Services We Offer

    We supply spare parts across all major onboard systems. Here is what Kontek Marine handles on a regular basis.

    Marine Engine Spare Parts

    Engine room performance depends on having the right parts available at the right time. As active Marine Engine Spare Parts Suppliers, we stock components for main engines, auxiliary engines, turbochargers, and generators across multiple major brands. OEM and compatible options are available based on urgency and budget.

    Genuine OEM Parts Sourcing

    When the original manufacturer part is required, Kontek Marine helps you Buy Genuine OEM Marine Spare Parts Online through verified supply channels. We confirm part numbers, check authenticity, and arrange direct shipment to your vessel or shore facility.

    Deck, Hull & Machinery Spares

    We supply materials of deck machinery, mooring equipment, steering systems, and hull fittings. Whether it is a single seal for a dry dock job or a batch order for a planned maintenance period, we handle it.

    Safety Equipment Spares

    Lifeboat engine parts, fire detection spares, breathing apparatus components, and immersion suit accessories. We stock these because safety equipment spares cannot wait for a long lead time.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Can I buy genuine OEM marine spare parts through Kontek Marine?

    Yes. We help clients Buy Genuine OEM Marine Spare Parts Online via authorised supply routes. Part numbers are confirmed before dispatch and supporting documentation is available on request.

    Does Kontek Marine supply spare parts outside India?

    Yes. Kontek Marine provides services globally. While we are headquartered in India, we supply Marine Engine Spare Parts to vessel operators and ship managers worldwide. We handle export documentation and international freight for all overseas orders.

    What types of vessels do you cover?

    We work across commercial ships, offshore support vessels, fishing boats, and workboats. As a Marine Spare Parts supplier, Kontek Marine covers everything from a single gasket right through to a full engine overhaul kit.

    Do you supply both OEM and compatible spare parts?

    Yes. Kontek Marine stocks both genuine OEM parts and quality-compatible alternatives. We always confirm which type is being supplied so you can make the right call based on your vessel's requirements and budget.

    Can you help identify a part if I only have a description?
