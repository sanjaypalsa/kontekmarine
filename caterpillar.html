    Caterpillar 314-7755 CDVR — CAT Digital Voltage Regulator

    The CAT 314-7755 CDVR is Caterpillar's advanced digital voltage regulator, designed to maintain precise and stable generator output voltage across varying load conditions. It replaces older analogue regulators with fully digital control, programmable voltage set points, and integrated diagnostic capability — making it ideal for modern CAT generator sets used in marine vessels, standby power systems, offshore platforms, and industrial prime power applications. Commonly replaced during scheduled CAT generator maintenance or after voltage instability faults.

    CAT 155-3832 Digital Voltage Regulator — Caterpillar Generator DVR

    The CAT 155-3832 is a standalone digital voltage regulator widely used across multiple generations of Caterpillar generator sets. It senses generator output voltage and automatically adjusts excitation current to maintain stable voltage output under changing loads. Adjustable sensing voltage, stability, and droop settings make it adaptable to different CAT genset configurations. Extensively used in marine generator sets, power plant standby generators, and industrial continuous power applications worldwide.

    The Caterpillar VR6 automatic voltage regulator (AVR), part number 365-2076, is engineered for CAT generator sets requiring reliable excitation control and stable output voltage under demanding operating conditions.     Part No. 227-5802-05

    Caterpillar EMCP 3.1, 3.2 & 3.3 Generator Controller Panel — 227-5802-05

    The Caterpillar EMCP 3 (Electric Modular Control Panel) is the industry-standard digital controller for CAT generator sets worldwide.     Part No. 234-1645

    Caterpillar 234-1645 RTD Module — 8-Channel Temperature Input

    The Caterpillar 234-1645 is an 8-channel RTD (Resistance Temperature Detector) input module used within CAT engine monitoring and control architectures. It reads temperature signals from Pt100 and Pt1000 RTD sensors mounted across the engine — covering coolant temperature, oil temperature, exhaust temperature, and ambient air temperature monitoring points. Accurate temperature monitoring is critical for protecting CAT engines from overheating faults and triggering the correct alarm and shutdown responses. Used in CAT marine propulsion engines, generator sets, and industrial power system installations.
