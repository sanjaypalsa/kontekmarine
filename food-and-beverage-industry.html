    Hygienic-Grade Parts & Reliable Maintenance for F&B Processing Operations

    Food and Beverage Solutions Processing lines, filling systems, packaging equipment, and conveyor systems infrastructure, we provide the technical support and spare parts to Protect your production continuity and food safety compliance.

    What We Offer: Food & Beverage

    Food and beverage manufacturing operates under stringent hygiene standards, strict production schedules, and zero tolerance for contamination risk. Equipment failures on a processing line don't just cost production time; they can compromise product safety, trigger regulatory responses, and damage brand reputation.

    Kontek Marine provides specialized Food & Beverage Spare Parts supply and maintenance services for food and beverage processing facilities, delivering technically verified components, hygienic-compatible materials, and structured maintenance programs that align with the safety and regulatory demands of this industry.

    Food and Beverage Services

    Processing Line Spare Parts Supply

    Procurement of food-grade components, including stainless steel fittings, hygienic sensors, pneumatic valves, pumps, sealing materials, and Food & Beverage Spare Parts for processing equipment. We supply reliable components that support safe food and beverage service operations while meeting material contact standards and maintaining smooth, efficient performance.

    Filling & Packaging Equipment Support

    Parts supply and maintenance support for filling machines, capping units, labelling systems, and wrapping lines. Our services cover mechanical, pneumatic, and electrical components to help reduce production interruptions, maintain reliable equipment performance, and support smooth packaging operations in food and beverage manufacturing environments.

    Conveyor & Handling System Maintenance

    Preventive Food Industry Maintenance Services and component support for conveyor systems used in the food and beverage industry. Our services include hygienic belt materials, stainless steel chain components, and wash-down-rated drive units designed to maintain reliable operation and support smooth processing in wet and dry environments.

    Pump & Valve System Services

    Sourcing and maintenance support for centrifugal pumps, diaphragm pumps, hygienic butterfly valves, and flow control components used in CIP process fluid handling systems. Our services help maintain smooth operation, improve hygiene standards, reduce equipment issues, and support reliable performance in food and beverage processing environments.

    Refrigeration & Cold Chain Equipment Support

    Parts supply and technical support for refrigeration compressors, condensers, expansion valves, and temperature control systems used in cold chain operations. Our services help maintain stable cooling performance, protect product quality, reduce equipment downtime, and support reliable processing and storage operations across temperature-sensitive environments.

    Preventive Maintenance Programs

    Scheduled Food Industry Maintenance Services include sanitation-compatible lubrication, seal replacement, sensor calibration, and equipment inspections to support hygiene compliance and reliable operation. Regular maintenance helps extend equipment life, reduce downtime, and maintain smooth production performance between processing runs in food manufacturing environments.

    KEY INDUSTRIES SERVED

    Food Processing Parts & Packaging Plants
    Beverage Bottling & Filling Facilities
    Dairy Processing Operations
    Confectionery & Bakery Manufacturing
    Cold Storage & Distribution Centers

    Why Kontek Marine for Food & Beverage

    Kontek Marine's approach to component traceability, documentation, and Technical accuracy is especially well-suited to food and beverage operations where compliance records and material certifications matter. We supply parts with full documentation where required, and maintain the rigorous standards Your industrial food processing systems are backed by experience in equally demanding marine environments and offshore environments.
