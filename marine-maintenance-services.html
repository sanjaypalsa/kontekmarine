    Reliable Maintenance & Repair For Ship Generators

    Introduction

    Kontek Marine is a Professional Marine Maintenance Services Company that has spent years working directly with vessel owners, fleet managers, and shipping operators across the globe.Our knowledge of this industry allows us to identify which factors enable business operations to function normally. The three essential elements for operational success include people with experience, authentic communication methods, and maintenance tasks that reach their completion without needing to be redone.

    The team operates between conducting single-vessel work and managing entire multiple-ship operations. Our approach to every assignment begins with our team arriving at the site to evaluate the requirements before proceeding to complete the work through proper methods. Our Marine Vessel & Commercial Ship Maintenance Services provide complete vessel maintenance through our single contact point which manages all aspects of vessel upkeep from hull maintenance through onboard system maintenance.

    We maintain 24-hour availability while our team can arrive at major ports within a 24-hour time frame. Our engineers stand ready to respond to both scheduled work and urgent situations. At Kontek Marine, we are not trying to sell you a package. We are here to keep your fleet running.

    Services We Offer

    We offer a focused range of marine engineering and maintenance services built around what ship operators actually need. From routine scheduled work to emergency repairs, here is what Kontek Marine does on a day-to-day basis.

    Planned & Preventive Marine Engineering Maintenance

    Preventing problems is always more cost-effective than fixing them later. Our Planned and Preventive Marine Engineering Maintenance programs follow OEM and class society guidelines, using your vessel’s history to schedule timely maintenance and avoid unexpected costs.

    • Main engine overhauls and cylinder liner inspections
    • Auxiliary machinery servicing and load testing
    • Fuel system cleaning and performance checks
    • Electrical panel maintenance and insulation testing
    • Ballast water treatment system servicing

    Marine Repair, Onboard Maintenance & Technical Support Services

    Breakdowns often happen at the worst times. Our Marine Repair, Onboard Maintenance, and Technical Support team responds quickly with the right spare parts and diagnostic tools. Our engineers are experienced in working under pressure and in tight spaces, starting work as soon as the vessel reaches dry dock, berth, or anchor.

    • Propulsion and steering system repairs
    • Onboard welding, fabrication, and structural work
    • Navigation and communication equipment repairs
    • HVAC and refrigeration system maintenance
    • Remote and onsite technical support, available 24/7

    Marine Vessel & Commercial Ship Maintenance Services

    Our Marine Vessel and Commercial Ship Maintenance Services support bulk carriers, tankers, container ships, RoRo ferries, and passenger vessels. We tailor maintenance plans to each vessel’s class, route, and operations while coordinating with classification societies and port authorities to keep certifications valid.

    • Maintenance support for bulk carriers, tankers, and container ships
    • RoRo ferry and passenger vessel maintenance services
    • Class-compliant inspection and servicing programs
    • Coordination with classification societies and port authorities
    • Certificate renewal support and compliance checks

    Marine Maintenance Services for Offshore Vessels

    Offshore locations are remote, conditions are demanding, and safety standards leave no room for shortcuts. Our Marine Maintenance Services for Offshore Vessels cover PSVs, AHTS vessels, accommodation units, and subsea construction ships, planned and delivered to the standards oil and gas operators require.

    • Maintenance services for PSV and AHTS vessels
    • Accommodation vessel and offshore support unit servicing
    • Subsea construction vessel maintenance support
    • Planned maintenance as per offshore safety standards
    • Inspection, repair, and performance testing for offshore equipment
    • Support aligned with oil & gas operator requirements

    Our Advantages

    There are a lot of marine maintenance companies out there. Here is what makes working with Kontek Marine a different experience.

    Qualified Engineers Who Know the Job

    Our team includes Class I and Class II marine engineers, certified welders, electrical specialists, and technicians with international certifications. When our engineers board a vessel, they already understand the systems they are working on.

    We Can Reach You, Wherever You Are

    Kontek Marine has service teams across major shipping routes and can usually reach your vessel within 24 to 48 hours, even at difficult locations.

    Maintenance Tracking That Actually Works

    We use a Planned Maintenance System to record every job, spare part, and service history. This supports our Planned & Preventive Marine Engineering Maintenance approach and gives fleet managers a clear view of upcoming and completed work.

    Compliance Without the Headache

    As a Professional Marine Maintenance Services Company, Kontek Marine handles IMO, SOLAS, MARPOL, MLC, ISM Code, and Port State Control requirements along with the technical work, so compliance stays under control.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    What types of vessels does Kontek Marine work on?

    We provide Marine Vessel and Commercial Ship Maintenance Services for container ships, bulk carriers, tankers, ferries, offshore support vessels, and industrial ships, including Marine Maintenance Services for Offshore Vessels and port-side fleet support.

    How does the Planned & Preventive Marine Engineering Maintenance programme work?

    We inspect the vessel, create a plan based on class and maker requirements, and track all work through a PMS so your team can see completed, pending, and urgent tasks.

    How fast can Kontek Marine get an engineer to our vessel?

    At major ports, our Marine Repair, Onboard Maintenance & Technical Support Services engineer can usually attend within 12–24 hours, and about 48 hours for remote locations.

    Can Kontek Marine help with class surveys and flag state compliance?

    Yes. Our Marine Vessel & Commercial Ship Maintenance Services support annual surveys, special surveys, and inspections, working with class societies like Lloyd’s, DNV, and Bureau Veritas.

    Do you offer long-term contracts for offshore fleet maintenance?

    Yes. Our Marine Maintenance Services for Offshore Vessels contracts provide ongoing support, stable costs, and a team familiar with your fleet.
