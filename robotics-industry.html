    Expert Spare Parts & Technical Support for Industrial Robotics Systems

    Exact component sourcing and maintenance services for industrial robot spare parts in manufacturing environments for robotics automation solutions. Servo drives, controllers, end-effectors, and more.

    What We Offer Robotics

    Industrial robotics needs absolute precision in its components, in its maintenance, and in the technical support they are given. When a servo drive fails or a replacement part is incompatible, the entire automated cell can be compromised, costing hours or days of production.

    Kontek Marine specializes in supplying spare parts and technical support for industrial robot spare parts systems using a worldwide supplier network and technical expertise developed in demanding operational environments. We know how important it is to keep robots up and running – and we do just that.

    Robotics Automation Services

    Robot Spare Parts Procurement

    Procurement service for industrial robot spare parts including servo motors, drives, encoders, teach pendants, control boards, wrist assemblies, and cable harnesses for major industrial robotics systems such as FANUC, ABB, KUKA, Yaskawa & Kawasaki. OEM and certified compatible components are supplied for reliable robotic system performance.

    Robot Controller Support

    Parts supply and technical assistance for robot controllers covering I/O modules, CPU cards, power supply units, and communication interfaces. As a trusted Industrial Robotics Company, we provide reliable support for controller maintenance, diagnostics, and system performance optimization. Backup and restore support for controller data where required.

    Servo Drive & Motor Replacement Support

    Component identification, sourcing, compatibility checks, and parameter matching.Our technical guidance helps ensure smooth installation, stable system performance, and reliable operation across industrial robotics applications while reducing downtime and maintaining overall equipment efficiency.

    Preventive Maintenance for Robotic Cells

    Our robotics maintenance services include joint inspections, battery checks, greasing, cable inspection, and system calibration to keep robotic production cells running smoothly. Regular maintenance helps reduce unexpected breakdowns, improve equipment reliability, and support safe, efficient production operations with consistent robotic system performance over time.

    End-of-Life Parts Sourcing

    Specialized procurement of industrial robot spare parts for discontinued robotic systems where manufacturers no longer supply components. We source reliable replacement parts through certified suppliers with full traceability to reduce downtime, maintain smooth operations, and support reliable performance in industrial robotic production environments.

    Emergency Robotic Parts Mobilization

    When a robotic cell fails unexpectedly, our team acts quickly to identify, source, and deliver the required components with minimal delay. We support industrial robotics operations by helping reduce downtime, restore production efficiency, and keep robotic systems running smoothly through fast response and reliable parts support.

    KEY INDUSTRIES SERVED

    Electronics Manufacturing Automation
    Palletizing & Pick-and-Place Operations
    Pharmaceutical & Medical Device Production
    Defence & Precision Manufacturing Facilities

    Why Kontek Marine for Robotics

    Our experience sourcing complex, critical components for marine systems where part failure at sea has serious consequences gives us the discipline and urgency required for robotic support. We verify compatibility, we document supply chains, and we deliver with accuracy. No guesswork. No substitutions without confirmation.
