    Marine Spare Parts Supplier for Northern Europe

    Kontek Marine supplies marine spare parts for vessels operating across Northern Europe, including Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Germany, the Netherlands, and the Baltic region. We support ship owners, ship managers, fleet operators, and marine purchasing teams with engine, generator, turbocharger, pump, valve, electrical, and automation spare parts.

    Whether you need planned maintenance or urgent vessel repairs, our team can identify and source the required component using the OEM part number, equipment model, nameplate information, drawings, or photographs.

    For a broader range of marine spare parts, explore our Marine Spare Parts Supplier Services.

    Marine Spare Parts Supply Across Northern Europe

    Northern Europe is an important maritime region connecting the North Sea, Baltic Sea and major European shipping routes. Vessels operating in this region include container ships, bulk carriers, tankers, ferries, offshore support vessels, tugs, workboats and other commercial vessels.

    These vessels require reliable access to replacement components for engines, generators, propulsion systems, pumps, valves and automation equipment.

    Kontek Marine supports spare parts requirements across key Northern European markets, including:

    • Norway - Bergen, Stavanger and other maritime and offshore locations
    • Sweden - Gothenburg, Stockholm and Baltic routes
    • Denmark - Copenhagen, Aarhus, and Esbjerg
    • Finland - Helsinki, Turku and other Baltic ports
    • Germany - Hamburg and North Sea/Baltic operations
    • Netherlands - Rotterdam and other major shipping locations
    • Baltic Region - Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania and Poland

    Our international sourcing capability allows customers to request parts for both common and specialized marine equipment.

    Marine Engine Spare Parts for Northern European Vessels

    Engine reliability is essential for commercial vessels operating on demanding shipping schedules. Kontek Marine supplies and sources components for main engines, auxiliary engines, and marine generators.

    Common requirements include:

    • Pistons and piston rings
    • Cylinder liners
    • Cylinder heads
    • Bearings
    • Fuel injectors and injection pumps
    • Exhaust valves
    • Gaskets and sealing kits
    • Turbocharger components
    • Cooling system components
    • Lubrication system parts
    • Sensors and control components

    We support requirements for multiple marine engine manufacturers, including MAN B&W, Wärtsilä, Caterpillar, Cummins, MTU, Yanmar, Volvo Penta, Daihatsu, Mitsubishi and other brands, subject to the specific equipment and part requirements.

    For Wärtsilä-related requirements, see our Wärtsilä Marine Spare Parts page.

    Main Engine Spare Parts

    Our sourcing scope can include pistons, cylinder liners, piston rings, bearings, connecting rod components, exhaust valves, fuel injection components, and gasket sets.

    Auxiliary Engine & Generator Parts

    We can source fuel system components, filters, pumps, injectors, cooling system parts, sensors, starter components, and generator control equipment.

    For generator repair, servicing and overhaul requirements, see our Marine Generator Service page.

    Turbocharger Spare Parts

    Depending on the make and model, requirements may include bearings, nozzle rings, compressor components, turbine components, sealing parts and repair kits.

    Pumps, Valves & Mechanical Components

    Marine vessels regularly require replacement parts for pumps and valves, including:

    • Impellers
    • Mechanical seals
    • Pump shafts
    • Wear rings
    • Valve seats
    • Spindles
    • Actuators
    • Gaskets and seals

    Marine Electrical & Automation Parts

    We also support marine automation and electrical requirements, including:

    • PLC components
    • Sensors
    • Control modules
    • HMIs
    • Drives
    • Relays
    • Power supply components
    • Monitoring equipment

    Our website also covers a broad range of marine automation and industrial brands used in vessel applications.

    OEM and Alternative Marine Spare Parts

    Depending on the equipment, application, and procurement requirement, vessel operators may require either genuine OEM components or suitable alternative parts.

    OEM spare parts may be preferred for critical equipment, manufacturer-specific applications, warranty requirements, or repairs where specified documentation is required.

    Alternative spare parts may be considered when technically compatible and suitable for the application, particularly when availability, lead time, or procurement cost is an important consideration.

    The correct choice should always be based on the equipment specifications, manufacturer requirements, and applicable vessel or class requirements.

    Kontek Marine can help customers identify available sourcing options based on the specific part number and equipment details.

    How to Order Marine Spare Parts for a Vessel

    Accurate information helps reduce delays during part identification and quotation.

    When requesting a marine spare part, provide as much of the following information as possible:

    • Vessel name
    • IMO number
    • Port of call
    • Vessel ETA
    • Equipment manufacturer
    • Equipment model
    • Engine serial number, where applicable
    • OEM part number
    • Required quantity
    • Nameplate information
    • Photograph or equipment drawing
    • Required delivery date
    • OEM or alternative requirement

    For urgent requirements, port of call and vessel ETA are especially important because delivery arrangements need to be considered alongside the vessel's schedule.

    Emergency Marine Spare Parts Support

    A failed engine, pump, valve or automation component can create a time-sensitive requirement for a vessel.

    For an urgent enquiry, send the vessel name, IMO number, equipment manufacturer, model, part number, quantity, current location, next port and ETA.

    Kontek Marine can then assess the requirement and coordinate sourcing and logistics based on the part's availability and destination.

    For broader technical repair and maintenance requirements, see our Marine Maintenance Services.

    Who supplies marine spare parts to Northern Europe?

    Kontek Marine supplies and sources marine spare parts for ship owners, ship managers, fleet operators and marine purchasing teams operating in Northern Europe and international markets.

    Can you supply marine spare parts to Norway, Sweden and Denmark?

    Do you supply marine engine spare parts?

    The most useful information includes the manufacturer, model, OEM part number, equipment serial number, vessel name, and photographs or nameplate details. Port of call and ETA are also useful for delivery planning.
