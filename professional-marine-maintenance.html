    Professional Marine Maintenance Services Company

    Keeping a fleet running rarely comes down to one big fix — it's the small problems caught early that keep ships out of dry dock and on schedule. Kontek Marine works as a Professional Marine Maintenance Services Company for vessel owners, fleet managers, and shipping operators who need a partner that shows up on time, does the job right the first time, and keeps compliance paperwork in order. Whether you operate one tanker or a fleet of twenty, our engineers focus on one outcome: less time in dry dock, more time earning at sea.

    Planned & Preventive Marine Engineering Maintenance

    Reactive maintenance is definitely going to be more expensive than preventive maintenance. Following OEM recommendations and guidelines from the relevant classification societies, we use your ship's maintenance history as an indication of where the potential problem areas may lie before the situation becomes a cause for downtime for your vessel.

    • Overhauling of the main engines, top-end repair, and cylinder liner inspection
    • Servicing of auxiliary engines and load test
    • Fuel system cleaning and combustion efficiency check
    • Electrical panel inspection and insulation resistance test
    • Maintaining the ballast water treatment system

    Marine Repair, Onboard Maintenance & Technical Support Services

    Breakdowns rarely happen at a convenient time. Our engineers mobilise with the right spare parts and diagnostic equipment, ready to start work the moment a vessel reaches berth, anchor, or dry dock — and they're just as comfortable working under pressure in tight spaces as they are talking an issue through over the phone first.

    • Propulsion and steering system repairs
    • Onboard welding, fabrication, and structural work
    • Navigation and communication equipment troubleshooting
    • HVAC and refrigeration system repairs
    • Remote diagnostics and 24/7 technical support

    Marine Vessel & Commercial Ship Maintenance Services

    Various types of ships require various risk assessments and certification procedures. Among the types of vessels we work with are bulk carriers, tankers, container ships, RoRo ferries, and passenger ships, all having tailored programs based on their type, itinerary, and survey cycle. We also work closely with classification bodies and port authorities to ensure that our clients’ certificates do not expire and that the inspections take them by surprise. You can browse the full scope of services we handle in-house before reaching out.

    • Maintenance services for bulk carriers, tankers, and container ships
    • Servicing of RoRo ferries and passenger ships
    • Class-based inspection programs
    • Certificate renewal and compliance assistance

    Marine Maintenance Services for Offshore Vessels

    Offshore work leaves no margin for shortcuts — locations are remote, conditions are harsher, and safety standards are stricter. We plan and deliver work for PSVs, AHTS vessels, accommodation units, and subsea construction ships to the standard that oil and gas operators expect, aligned with international safety requirements set by the International Maritime Organization.

    • Maintenance for PSV and AHTS vessels
    • Accommodation unit and offshore support vessel servicing
    • Subsea construction vessel maintenance support
    • Inspection, repair, and performance testing for offshore equipment

    Why Operators Choose Kontek Marine

    There's no shortage of companies offering marine maintenance — the difference shows up in how a job is actually run. As a Professional Marine Maintenance Services Company, Kontek Marine combines Class I and Class II certified engineers, certified welders, and electrical specialists who already understand the systems they're boarding to work on, so less time gets spent figuring things out on-site.

    Pricing is broken down before work starts, labour, parts, and travel costs are itemised, so there are no surprises on the final invoice. Every technician operates under a strict HSEQ framework with a zero-harm policy, and our Planned Maintenance System logs every job and spare part, giving fleet managers a clear record of what's been done and what's coming up next.

    • Service teams positioned across major shipping routes, reaching most vessels in 24–48 hours
    • Transparent, itemised quotes with no hidden costs
    • A single point of contact instead of juggling multiple contractors
    • Compliance handled alongside the technical work — IMO, SOLAS, MARPOL, MLC, and ISM Code
    • A documented track record across hundreds of vessel maintenance projects worldwide

    Maintenance work in the marine industry should be organized to achieve optimal outcomes. Kontek Marine employs the services of certified marine engineers, along with fair pricing and quick turnaround, to ensure that your vessel stays up to code and runs smoothly. No matter whether you have one vessel or many, Kontek Marine offers customized marine maintenance options.

    Contact our team today for a detailed, itemized quotation and discover how proactive marine maintenance can reduce costs while improving vessel reliability.

    Conclusion

    Marine maintenance works best when it's planned, not improvised. Kontek Marine pairs certified engineers, transparent pricing, and fast response times to keep vessels compliant and running, whatever the vessel type or location. From routine servicing to offshore emergencies, our team treats every job like it's our own fleet at stake.

    FAQs Kontek Marine Blog

    How quickly can Kontek Marine reach our vessel?

    At major ports, our engineers typically arrive within 24 hours; remote or offshore locations may take up to 48 hours, depending on access.

    Does Kontek Marine handle class surveys and compliance paperwork?

    Yes, we work alongside classification societies such as DNV and Lloyd's Register to support annual surveys, special surveys, and certificate renewals.

    Can you support long-term offshore fleet contracts?

    Yes, our offshore maintenance contracts are built for ongoing support, with a team that stays familiar with your fleet rather than starting fresh each visit.
