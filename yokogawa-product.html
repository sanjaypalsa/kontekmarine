    Yokogawa Zirconia Cell E7042UD

    The Yokogawa E7042UD is a high-precision zirconia oxygen sensor cell designed for in-situ measurement of oxygen concentration in combustion flue gas and industrial process streams. It functions as the core electrochemical sensing element within Yokogawa's ZR-series analyzer probes, generating an EMF signal proportional to oxygen concentration via solid zirconia electrolyte technology. The cell delivers stable, long-life performance and is a direct replacement component for Yokogawa oxygen analyzer systems used in power plant boilers, industrial furnaces, marine exhaust systems, and continuous emissions monitoring (CEMS) installations.
