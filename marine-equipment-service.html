    Reliable Maintenance & Repair For Ship Generators

    Introduction

    Kontek Marine is one of the established marine equipment suppliers & distributors in India, working with shipping companies, shipyards, vessel operators, and offshore contractors across the country. We focus on providing the right equipment on time, supported by people who understand marine operations and real onboard requirements.

    India has established itself as an important maritime center which requires dependable marine engineering and equipment services. Kontek Marine provides equipment which includes navigation and communication and electrical and engineering systems together with technical support which enables vessels to obtain their required equipment.

    We operate as a full service business that goes beyond our catalog offerings. When you contact Kontek Marine, you speak with someone who knows the industry. We provide straightforward and effective service for both urgent spare part requests and complete equipment package orders.

    Services We Offer

    Our services cover equipment supply, technical support, and after-sales care. Here is a breakdown of what Kontek Marine provides to vessel operators and maritime businesses across India.

    Marine Engineering & Equipment Supply

    As a marine engineering & equipment company, we supply a broad range of deck, engine room, and safety equipment to commercial vessels, offshore platforms, and naval support ships. Our product inventory is sourced from approved manufacturers and includes both OEM and compatible alternatives depending on the vessel's requirements and budget.

    Marine Navigation and Communication Equipment

    Marine navigation equipment needs complete accuracy because any failure can lead to maritime disasters. Kontek Marine supplies type-approved navigation and communication systems from leading brands which include bridge instrumentation and distress signalling equipment. We work with vessel masters and fleet technical teams to make sure the equipment matches the vessel class, trading area, and SOLAS requirements.

    Marine Electrical & Electronic Equipment

    Kontek Marine is a marine electrical & electronic equipment supplier covering a full range of electrical components for vessel power systems, control panels, and instrumentation. Electrical failures at sea create significant dangers. Our company maintains a selection of high-quality components from established brands which we use to provide system upgrade recommendations for outdated equipment that causes operational issues.

    Marine Equipment Service and After-Sales Support

    Supply does not end at delivery. Kontek Marine operates as a marine equipment service company that stays involved after the equipment reaches your vessel. Our technical team handles installation support, commissioning assistance, calibration, and periodic servicing for the equipment we supply. We also help coordinate warranty claims and sourcing of replacement parts when needed.

    Marine Equipment Distribution Across India

    As active marine equipment suppliers & distributors in India, we supply to ports and vessels across Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Kolkata, Goa, Kandla, and other key maritime locations. We have logistics arrangements in place to move equipment quickly, including time-sensitive port deliveries and bonded warehouse dispatch for vessels on tight turnaround schedules.

    Our Advantages

    Here is what working with Kontek Marine actually looks like compared to dealing with a general trading company or an overseas supplier.

    We Know the Equipment We Sell

    Kontek Marine functions as a marine engineering & equipment company, not just a parts stockist. Our team includes engineers who have worked at sea and in shipyards. When you ask a technical question, you get a useful answer, not a product code and a brochure.

    Stock That Moves Fast

    We maintain ready stock of fast-moving marine electrical & electronic equipment and navigation items at our India-based facilities. For vessels calling at Indian ports, this means same-day or next-day supply is genuinely possible for most standard items. We do not make you wait two weeks for something we already have on the shelf.

    Type-Approved and Certified Products

    Every piece of marine navigation and communication equipment we supply carries the relevant type approvals and certifications. We only work with manufacturers whose products meet IMO, SOLAS, and classification society standards. You will not receive equipment from us that creates problems during a Port State Control inspection.

    End-to-End Support

    As a marine equipment service company, Kontek Marine does not hand over equipment and walk away. We support you through installation, commissioning, and routine servicing. If something goes wrong under warranty, we handle it. Our goal is for the equipment to work properly and for you not to have to chase multiple people when it does not.

    Why Choose Kontek Marine?

    There are several marine equipment supply company options operating in India. Here is a plain answer to why clients choose us and stay with us.

    As marine equipment suppliers & distributors in India, we have the local infrastructure to deliver to any major Indian port quickly and without the complications that come with importing from abroad.

    We cover navigation, communication, electrical, and engineering equipment under one roof. As a marine equipment supply company, we reduce the number of vendors you need to manage.

    Our engineers and product specialists are reachable during and after business hours. For urgent supply or installation queries, we do not leave you waiting on hold.

    Ship managers, shipping lines, offshore companies, and shipyards have used Kontek Marine as their marine electrical & electronic equipment supplier for years. The repeat business reflects the reliability of our supply and service.

    If a product is not right for your application, we will tell you. We would rather lose a small sale than have you come back with a problem three months later.

    Our quotes are clear and itemised. Freight, customs handling, and delivery costs are laid out upfront. What we quote is what you pay.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Is Kontek Marine a manufacturer or a distributor?

    Kontek Marine is a marine equipment supply company and authorised distributor. We work with trusted manufacturers to provide a wide range of marine equipment without clients needing multiple suppliers.

    Does Kontek Marine supply equipment outside India?

    Yes. As one of the marine equipment suppliers & distributors in India, we supply to vessels and companies across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Europe, including export handling and logistics support.

    Do you supply type-approved marine navigation and communication equipment?

    Yes. All marine navigation and communication equipment we supply comes with required type approvals and documentation for class surveys and inspections.

    Can Kontek Marine handle installation of the equipment it supplies?

    Yes. As a marine equipment service company, we provide installation and commissioning support for electrical, navigation, and engineering equipment at port or during dry dock.

    How long does delivery take for equipment ordered from Kontek Marine?

    Delivery depends on stock availability. Items in India can ship in 1–3 days, while sourced equipment is delivered as per confirmed lead time with full logistics support.

    What makes Kontek Marine different from other marine equipment companies in India?

    Kontek Marine combines supply and service. As a marine engineering & equipment company, we provide the right equipment, technical support, and fast response when vessels need urgent assistance.
