    Reliable Maintenance & Repair For Ship Generators

    Introduction

    Kontek Marine leads the field in Marine & Maritime Ship Fleet Modernization Services, setting the standard for how vessel upgrades are planned, managed, and delivered. We work with some of the most demanding operators in the industry and bring that same level of precision and accountability to every project, regardless of vessel size or fleet complexity.

    Fleet operators who choose to modernize their existing systems instead of complete replacement because it brings them financial benefits. Our Marine Vessel & Ship Modernization Services exist to make that decision pay off. We start our projects with a complete technical blueprint and our skilled engineers and our power to manage all project elements from class approval through to final commissioning.

    Kontek Marine has a strong record of delivering modernization projects on time and within agreed budgets across commercial vessels, offshore support ships, and passenger ferries in India and internationally. When we take on a project, we own it completely and deliver it to the standard our clients expect.

    Services We Offer

    Our modernization scope covers the full vessel from engine room to bridge. Here is what Kontek Marine handles as part of our Marine Fleet Upgrade & Refurbishment Solutions for Ships.

    Engine Room Upgrades & Machinery Modernization

    Many vessels lose significant fuel efficiency as machinery ages and tolerances widen. We carry out engine room upgrades covering main engine reconditioning, exhaust gas boiler replacements, fuel injection system overhauls, and auxiliary upgrades. Work is planned around class intervals wherever possible to avoid additional off-hire periods.

    Bridge & Navigation System Replacement

    Older navigation and communication suites are a compliance risk and a practical limitation. We replace and upgrade radar systems, ECDIS units, AIS transponders, VHF and GMDSS equipment, and bridge alarm management systems. All equipment supplied is type-approved and installed by qualified technicians.

    Emissions Compliance Retrofits

    Emissions compliance is no longer optional. Our vessel upgrade programme includes ballast water treatment system retrofits, scrubber installations, and selective catalytic reduction unit fitting. We manage the full project including class approval drawings, equipment supply, installation, and commissioning.

    Accommodation & Crew Welfare Refurbishment

    Crew retention is linked directly to accommodation standards. We handle full cabin refurbishments, mess room upgrades, galley equipment replacement, and recreational area improvements as part of our Commercial Fleet Modernization services. Work is scoped to MLC requirements and completed during scheduled dry dock periods.

    Electrical System Modernization

    The project requires the execution of electrical system upgrades together with switchboard replacements and LED lighting conversions and the implementation of automated systems. The process begins with an evaluation of your vessel's electrical load to find existing inefficiencies which we will address through proposed upgrades that provide future cost savings.

    Our Advantages

    Here is what makes Kontek Marine the right choice for fleet modernization work.

    We Plan Around Your Trading Schedule

    Off-hire time costs money. Every fleet upgrade and refurbishment project we take on is planned to fit your vessel's trading pattern. We coordinate with shipyards, class surveyors, and equipment suppliers in advance so delays on one part of the job do not push everything else back.

    Class Approval Is Part of the Job

    Our engineers handle technical drawings, approval submissions, and class liaison as part of the project scope. You do not need to manage that separately. We have done this enough times to know what class surveyors look for and how to get approvals through without unnecessary rework.

    One Team from Start to Finish

    Some contractors hand off to different teams mid-project. With Kontek Marine, the same technical team that surveys your vessel also manages the work and signs off the completion. This matters when problems come up mid-job, because the people making decisions know the full picture.

    Experience Across Vessel Types

    Our modernization experience covers bulk carriers, tankers, offshore support vessels, RoRo ships, and passenger ferries. We are not learning on your vessel. We have handled similar projects before and know where the complications tend to appear.

    From initial survey and scope definition through class approval, shipyard coordination, and sea trials, we manage the entire process.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    What does Kontek Marine's fleet modernization scope cover?

    It covers any planned upgrade or improvement to an existing vessel's systems, spaces, or equipment. The project encompasses engine room upgrades and bridge system replacements and emissions compliance retrofits and accommodation refurbishments and electrical system improvements. Kontek Marine scopes each project individually based on the vessel's condition and your operational requirements.

    How long does a typical fleet modernization project take?

    It depends on the scope. A focused bridge upgrade can be completed in a week alongside a port call. A full vessel upgrade and refurbishment project covering multiple systems typically requires a planned dry dock of two to four weeks. We give you a realistic timeline after the initial vessel survey.

    Can Kontek Marine handle modernization for an entire fleet?

    Yes. Our fleet modernization programmes are structured to handle multiple vessels in sequence or in parallel depending on your schedule and budget. We manage the programme at a fleet level so specifications stay consistent across vessels and procurement is handled efficiently.

    Do you work with vessels outside India?

    Yes. Kontek Marine delivers vessel and ship modernization work globally. We have delivered projects at shipyards in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Europe. Our team travels to the vessel and manages local subcontractors and suppliers where needed.

    How do you handle class approval for modernization work?

    We manage class approval as part of the project. This includes preparing technical drawings, submitting documentation to the relevant classification society, attending flag state meetings where required, and ensuring all installation work meets the approved specifications. We work with all major class societies including Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas, and ClassNK.
