    Global Marine Services Provider

    Kontek Marine is a trusted marine services company, supporting ship owners, operators, and fleet managers with dependable spare parts, engineering, and technical support across major Indian ports and international waters. As a global marine services provider, we bring together deep technical expertise, a wide OEM and compatible spare parts inventory, and a responsive engineering team to keep vessels safe, compliant, and operational. Whether you need a single spare part delivered against a tight port call or complete offshore marine services for a fleet, Kontek Marine is built to respond quickly and deliver work you can rely on.

    About Kontek Marine A Trusted Marine Equipment Service Company

    Kontek Marine operates as a specialized marine equipment service company, based in New Delhi, serving ship owners, ship managers, and offshore operators across India and abroad. Our team combines hands-on engineering experience with a structured approach to inspection, maintenance, and spare parts supply, drawing on established relationships with leading equipment brands including Wärtsilä, ABB, Honeywell, Woodward, Flender, SICK, and Fanuc.

    We work to international maritime regulations and classification society standards, including IMO, DNV, Lloyd's Register, and Bureau Veritas, so that every service we deliver supports your vessel's compliance and seaworthiness. From routine servicing to urgent breakdown response, Kontek Marine has completed more than 2,500 projects for over 2,700 customers, backed by a team of 60+ marine engineers and technicians.

    FAQs Kontek Marine Services

    What makes Kontek Marine a trusted marine services company?

    Kontek Marine combines hands-on engineering experience, a wide spare parts inventory, and compliance with IMO, DNV, Lloyd's Register, and Bureau Veritas standards, giving ship owners and operators a dependable single point of contact for maintenance, repair, and spare parts across Indian ports.

    Does Kontek Marine supply genuine OEM spare parts?

    Yes. As a Marine Spare Parts Supplier India shipowners rely on, we supply OEM and quality-compatible spare parts across major engine, generator, and automation brands with fast delivery for urgent requirements.

    Can Kontek Marine handle emergency ship repairs?

    Yes. Our ship repair and maintenance team responds quickly to emergency and unplanned breakdowns, working to minimize vessel downtime and operational losses at Indian ports.

    What does fleet modernization involve?

    Fleet modernization services include machinery upgrades, system integration, structural improvements, and energy efficiency enhancements that help vessels meet current compliance and performance requirements without a full asset replacement.

    Does Kontek Marine provide offshore support as well as onboard services?

    Yes. Our offshore marine services cover equipment inspection, troubleshooting, and engineering support for offshore assets and industrial marine operations, in addition to our onboard vessel services.
